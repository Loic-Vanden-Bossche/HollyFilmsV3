# Google G

Official gradient Google G, downloaded from Google's [Partner Marketing Hub](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/branding-guidelines/how-to-show-googles-brand/).

Original asset: `GoogleG_FullColor_RGB.png`. Preserve its colors and proportions.

`google-gradient.png` is the 2820 × 2820 master. The sign-in buttons use `google-gradient-96.png`, cropped to the original 940 × 940 viewport at (940, 940) and downsampled with Lanczos filtering in premultiplied alpha. Both render the mark at its original proportions.
